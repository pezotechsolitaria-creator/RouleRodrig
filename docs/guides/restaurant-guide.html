Running your kitchen

Everything the restaurant screen does, and what Roulé Rodrigues can do for you.

For kitchen staff and owners · roulerodrig.com/kitchen

Your kitchen has its own screen. It shows the orders that belong to your restaurant only, and it needs no installation — it opens in the phone browser you already use. Prop the phone up where you cook and leave it open.

You never have to wait for Roulé Rodrigues to accept an order for you. Payments, cooking and cancelling are all yours. That is the whole point of this screen.

1. Getting in

1Ask to be added

Roulé Rodrigues adds your email address to your kitchen's team. You get an email saying you are on the kitchen team.

2Create your account with the SAME email

Tap Create your account in that email. Your access is matched on the address, so signing up with a different one leaves you locked out.

3Add it to your home screen

Once you are in, use your browser's Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.

"Not on a kitchen team yet"? You signed up with a different email than the invited one, or you are on a different restaurant's team than the one taking the orders. Send Roulé Rodrigues the exact address you used.

Turn the sound on — do this first, once

Tap Turn on sound for new orders at the top. The phone then chimes twice whenever an order arrives, and a yellow line says how many are new. Phones block sound until you tap something, which is why it cannot simply be on already. It remembers your choice.

The clock tells you what is urgent

Orders sit oldest first, so the top of the screen is always the next thing to cook. The screen stays awake on its own while it is open.

3. Money

Cash orders — cook straight away

A cash order arrives ready to cook. The customer pays you at the counter. When it is ready you will see "Take payment when you hand this over".

Bank transfers — you decide

When a customer pays by bank transfer they upload a photo of the transfer. The card shows "The customer says they have paid by bank transfer" with three choices:

  • View their proof of payment — opens the photo. Always look before accepting.
  • Paid in full — the whole amount arrived. The order goes to the kitchen.
  • Only part of it arrived… — type what you actually received. The rest becomes cash to collect on handover.
  • Not received — say why. The order is not cancelled; the customer can send a better photo.

The proof of payment is kept. You can reopen it at any time, on any order, including after you accept it and including after you reject it. If a payment is ever disputed, the photo is still there.

Money still owed

If part of an order is still to be paid in cash, a red box says Collect Rs X in cash. It tells you whether the customer collects it or the driver takes it at the door. When the money is in your hand, tap Cash received.

Do not tap "Cash received" before you have the money. It is a separate button from handing the food over precisely because the two do not always happen at the same moment.

4. Cooking an order through

The buttonWhat it means
Start cookingYou have seen it and started. The customer is told it is being prepared.
Food is readyIt is finished. The customer is emailed, and if it is a delivery a driver is called.
Handed to customerIt has left your counter. This closes the order.

If you tap the wrong button

Under the card there is a small Undo "Start cooking" or Undo "Food is ready". It puts the order back one step.

Handed to customer cannot be undone — the food has gone and the order is closed. If that was a mistake, phone Roulé Rodrigues.

Cancelling

Cancel this order sits at the bottom, small and quiet, because it is rare. You must give a reason and the customer sees it. If they had already paid, the screen tells you plainly that Roulé Rodrigues must return their money — you do not refund from this screen.

Nothing disappears

Finished, cancelled and refunded orders stay under "finished today — tap to review" at the bottom for the rest of the day.

5. Today's menu

The second tab is your menu for today. Three controls per dish:

ControlUse it when
Mark sold outYou have run out. Comes back on by itself tomorrow.
Take off the menuYou are not making it at all for now. Stays off until you put it back.
Set today's countYou are making a fixed number. It stops selling at that number.

Each dish shows how many sold today. Prices are not on this screen — those belong to the owner. To change a price, or to add or remove a dish, ask Roulé Rodrigues.

6. What Roulé Rodrigues can do for you

If you would rather phone than tap, they can do all of it from their own screen — take the order through, confirm a payment, set a dish sold out, change a count. You are never blocked because nobody is near the phone.

7. If something looks wrong

The screen says "Reconnecting…"
The phone has lost the internet. What you see may be out of date. It reconnects by itself.
No sound when orders arrive
Tap Turn on sound for new orders. Check the phone is not on silent.
An order I expected is missing
Check the restaurant name on the cards — you may be on a different kitchen's team. If the whole screen is empty it names which kitchen is quiet.
A bank transfer order shows nothing to cook
It says "Waiting for the customer's bank transfer". Nothing has been proven yet. Do not cook it.
I rejected a payment by mistake
Nothing is lost. The photo is still there and the order is still live — accept it.
